# CYCLONE-X: Scientific Data Sources & Telemetry Specification

## 1. Overview & Scientific Integrity Policy

The **CYCLONE-X** platform strictly separates observational ground truth from experimental model forecasts. In accordance with zero-fabrication standards:
- **No live satellite or radar observations are fabricated.**
- **No official warnings from the India Meteorological Department (IMD) or RSMC New Delhi are fabricated.**
- External providers that require authentication report their genuine configuration state (`NOT_CONFIGURED`, `ACTIVE`, `UNAVAILABLE`).
- Unconfigured feeds operate in verifiable **DEMO** or **HISTORICAL** replay mode with explicit watermarks.

---

## 2. Supported Data Sources & Adapters

### A. MOSDAC / ISRO (Space Applications Centre)
- **Satellites**: INSAT-3D, INSAT-3DR, INSAT-3DS (Geostationary meteorological satellites positioned over 74°E / 82°E).
- **Sensors**: Multi-spectral Imager (6 channels) & Sounder (19 channels).
- **Products Supported**:
  - `L1B_TIR1`: Thermal Infrared 1 (10.3 - 11.3 µm) for cloud-top temperature and deep convective core structure.
  - `L1B_TIR2`: Thermal Infrared 2 (11.5 - 12.5 µm) for split-window moisture differential analysis.
  - `L1B_MIR`: Middle Infrared (3.7 - 3.9 µm) for low-cloud and nocturnal tracking.
  - `L1B_WV`: Water Vapor (6.5 - 7.1 µm) for upper-tropospheric steering currents.
  - `L1B_VIS`: Visible (0.55 - 0.75 µm, 1 km resolution) for daylight center localization.
  - `L2B_HEM`: Hydro-Estimator precipitation rate (mm/hr).
  - `L2B_OLR`: Outgoing Longwave Radiation ($W/m^2$).
- **Configuration**: Requires `MOSDAC_API_KEY` and `MOSDAC_API_URL`.
- **Zero-Fabrication Status**: When credentials are absent, adapter reports `NOT_CONFIGURED` and provides verified historical benchmark granules (e.g. Super Cyclonic Storm Amphan 2020).

### B. India Meteorological Department (IMD / RSMC New Delhi)
- **Agency**: Regional Specialized Meteorological Centre (RSMC) for Tropical Cyclones Over the North Indian Ocean.
- **Products Supported**:
  - `BEST_TRACK_ARCHIVE`: Verified post-season best-track datasets (6-hour synoptic intervals with lat, lon, MSLP, 10-min sustained winds, and intensity classification).
  - `RSMC_TROPICAL_WEATHER_OUTLOOK`: Daily/bi-daily synoptic development advisories.
  - `SPECIAL_TROPICAL_CYCLONE_BULLETIN`: Official cyclone warnings.
- **Safety Decoupling**: Historical IMD best-track archives are strictly tagged as `HISTORICAL DATA` and are **never** displayed as active cyclone warnings.

### C. NASA GPM / IMERG (Global Precipitation Measurement)
- **Agencies**: NASA Goddard Space Flight Center & JAXA.
- **Product**: `GPM_3IMERGHH` (Integrated Multi-satellitE Retrievals for GPM).
- **Cadence & Resolution**: 0.1° x 0.1° (~10 km) gridded, half-hourly temporal resolution.
- **Runs**: Early (4-hr latency), Late (14-hr latency), Final (3.5-month research grade).
- **Configuration**: Requires NASA Earthdata Login via `NASA_GPM_USERNAME` and `NASA_GPM_PASSWORD`.
- **Zero-Fabrication Status**: Reports `NOT_CONFIGURED` when credentials are absent.

### D. Historical Track Archives (IBTrACS & WMO)
- **Formats**: CSV and JSON.
- **Fields Validated**: `storm_id`, `storm_name`, `timestamp`, `latitude` [-90, 90], `longitude` [-180, 180], `wind_speed` [0, 350 kts], `central_pressure` [800, 1050 hPa], `classification`.
- **Status**: Always `AVAILABLE` for historical dataset ingestion and evaluation.

### E. User Uploaded Satellite Imagery
- **Formats**: PNG, JPEG, TIFF (up to 15 MB).
- **Validation**: Strict MIME and magic-number verification; safe UUID filename generation to prevent directory traversal.
- **Classification**: Tagged explicitly as `USER UPLOADED DATA` with PIL dimension and file size extraction.

## 3. Scientific Data Modes

Every observation and forecast record in CYCLONE-X carries a mandatory `data_mode` field:

| Data Mode | Meaning | Visual Identifier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `DEMO` | Curated synthetic or historical benchmark scenarios (e.g., Amphan 2020, Biparjoy 2023) | Blue badge `DEMO DATA` |
| `HISTORICAL` | Official archived ground truth from IMD or IBTrACS | Purple badge `HISTORICAL DATA` |
| `USER_UPLOAD` | Satellite raster or CSV track points uploaded directly by user | Emerald badge `USER UPLOADED DATA` |
| `LIVE_NRT` | Real-time authenticated sensor feeds (only when provider API key is verified) | Cyan badge `LIVE / NRT` |
| `MODEL_PREDICTION` | Experimental forecast generated by CYCLONE-X ML models | Amber badge `MODEL PREDICTION` |
| `OFFICIAL_INFO` | Official text bulletin published by authorized national weather service | Gold badge `OFFICIAL INFO` |

## 4. Scientific Disclaimer

> **EXPERIMENTAL RESEARCH SYSTEM — NOT AN OFFICIAL WEATHER FORECAST.**
> CYCLONE-X is an experimental academic and decision-support research system. It does not issue official warnings or supersede the statutory weather forecasts issued by the India Meteorological Department (IMD), Regional Specialized Meteorological Centre (RSMC) New Delhi, or the National Hurricane Center (NHC).
